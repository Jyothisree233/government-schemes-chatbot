How SchemeAI Works

A simple three-step process to fetch personalized government aid recommendations.

01

Demographic Profiling

Tell the chatbot about your age, gender, occupation, location, and income class in natural language.

02

Semantic Parsing

Our AI extraction engine isolates eligibility triggers and compares them to our scheme databases.

03

Direct Recommendations

Receive list of eligible central/state schemes along with direct link coordinates to official apply forms.
