Popular Government Schemes

Explore key welfare initiatives managed across central and state ministries.

Farmer

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides an income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments to landholding farmer families across India.

Student

PM Vidyalaxmi

Offers seamless educational loans and interest subvention opportunities for students wishing to pursue higher education in premium national institutions.

View Details Portal
Healthcare

The world's largest health assurance scheme providing a comprehensive health cover of ₹5 Lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization.
