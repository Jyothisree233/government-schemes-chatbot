How SchemeAI Works

Precision retrieval combined with modern generative reasoning.

1. Semantic Search (S-BERT)

Even if you don't know official scheme names, our Sentence-BERT embeddings match your natural words—in English or Hindi—with verified policies.

2. Vector Indexing (FAISS)

Lightning-fast sub-millisecond retrieval locates the top matching national and state schemes across our catalog of 90+ verified government schemes.

3. Generative AI (Gemini)

Google Gemini synthesizes eligibility criteria, document requirements, and step-by-step application instructions into friendly, direct advice.
